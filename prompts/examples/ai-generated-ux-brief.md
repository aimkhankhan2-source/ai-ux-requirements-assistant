# AI-Generated UX Requirements Example

## Input

> University students struggle to find available study rooms.

## Problem

Students need a simple way to discover and reserve available study rooms.

## Target Users

University students looking for study spaces.

## Pain Points

- Difficulty finding available rooms
- Unclear room capacity
- Limited information about equipment
- Uncertainty about available time slots

## User Goals

- Find a suitable room quickly
- Check availability
- Compare room options
- Reserve a room
- Manage bookings

## Functional Requirements

- Search rooms
- Filter rooms
- Display availability
- Display room details
- Book a room
- Cancel a booking
- View booking history
